Net Spend

Privacy policy

Last updated 10 November 2026

Net Spend collects nothing about you itself. It can show adverts, served by Google, and where the law asks for it Google only uses data about you with your consent. This page explains what happens, what you can choose, and your rights under UK data protection law.

The short version. No account. No analytics. No notifications. Adverts come from Google AdMob: before any advert is requested you are asked for your consent where UK law requires it, then whether to allow tracking. You can change your mind at any time. When the app has a sponsor, the adverts are switched off.

1. Who we are

Net Spend is an independent app that shows what each football club has spent and received in transfer fees, season by season, from Two-Footed Tackle's record of every transfer. It is made with Two-Footed Tackle, the football finance newsletter, and published by PickleSoft, a trading name of James Constant, who for the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018 is the data controller for the app.

To contact us about this policy or about your data, email support@picklesoft.co.uk.

2. What the app itself collects

Nothing. We do not collect, receive or store any personal data about you.

To show you the figures, the app reads one published set of figures from our data service, hosted by Supabase, and when it opens it asks the same service whether adverts or a sponsor are switched on. Neither request carries an account, a name, a device identifier or anything about what you look at. The same answer goes to everyone.

Like any server, the data service briefly records the IP address a request came from, to run the service and protect it from abuse. We do not use those records to identify you, link them to anything else, or keep them beyond the hosting provider's standard log period.

3. Adverts

When adverts are switched on, the app shows them through Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC: a banner at the foot of some pages, an advert in some lists, an occasional full-screen advert after you close a share card, and an advert you can choose to watch to open Compare for the day. Every advert is marked as one.

Your consent comes first. Before any advert is requested, the app shows Google's consent form where UK or EU law requires it. You can accept or decline personalised adverts. If you decline, you may still see adverts, but they are not based on data about you.

Tracking is your choice. Your iPhone then asks whether to allow the app to track you. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, Google does not receive your device's advertising identifier.

What Google receives. To show, count and protect adverts, Google's software in the app collects information such as your IP address (and so your rough location), your device model and system version, the app's identity, how adverts are seen and tapped, and, only if you allowed tracking, your device's advertising identifier. Google uses it to choose and measure adverts, limit how often you see the same one and prevent fraud, and, if you consented, to personalise adverts. How Google handles it is set out in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's privacy policy. Google may process it outside the UK under its own safeguards.

Our lawful basis for personalised adverts is your consent. We do not receive the information Google collects about you; we see only totals, such as how many adverts were shown.

Adverts are limited to general audiences, and categories unsuitable for the app, such as gambling, are blocked.

Changing your mind. Ad privacy choices, at the foot of the home screen and of About Net Spend, reopens Google's consent form. You can change tracking at any time in your iPhone's Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking.

4. Sponsors

When Net Spend has a sponsor, the adverts are switched off and the sponsor's name appears in the app and on the cards you share. Showing a sponsor's name involves no data about you. If you tap it, the sponsor's website opens in your browser, where its own privacy policy applies.

5. What is stored on your device

One thing is saved on your own phone, in the app's private storage: the day you last watched an advert to open Compare, so it stays open until midnight. Google's consent software keeps your consent choices on your phone so it does not ask every time. The figures you have looked at are kept in the app's memory while it is open and are gone when it closes. None of this is sent to us, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

6. Links

Links in the app, such as to Two-Footed Tackle or a news report, open in your browser, outside the app, where that website's own privacy policy applies.

7. Payment

Net Spend is free. If a one-off purchase to remove adverts is added, it will be made through Apple, who handle the payment; we never see your card details.

8. Notifications

The app sends no notifications and never asks for notification permission.

9. Children

The app is rated suitable for all ages and is not directed at children. Adverts are limited to general audiences.

10. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time.

We hold no personal data about you. For the information Google collects to serve adverts, you can withdraw consent through Ad privacy choices, turn off tracking in your iPhone's Settings, and use the controls described in Google's privacy policy. If you believe we hold data about you, email support@picklesoft.co.uk and we will respond within one month.

If you are unsatisfied with how a request is handled, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

11. Other services we operate

PickleSoft publishes other apps and websites, and Two-Footed Tackle publishes its newsletter; each has its own privacy policy. This policy covers Net Spend only. Using this app does not sign you up to, or share anything with, anything else we run.

12. Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated before that change ships, and the date at the top will say when.

Net Spend is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any football club, league or governing body. Club names are used to identify recorded transfers and nothing more. Net Spend is not financial or investment advice.
