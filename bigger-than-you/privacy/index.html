Bigger Than You

Privacy policy

Last updated 29 October 2026

Bigger Than You collects nothing about you, because it asks for nothing and sends nothing anywhere. This page explains that in full, and sets out your rights under UK data protection law.

The short version. No account. No servers. No analytics. No adverts. No tracking. No notifications. The entire record is inside the app when you download it, so nothing you do in it leaves your phone.

1. Who we are

Bigger Than You is an independent app that rates every English club on where it has finished in the league since 1888. It is published by PickleSoft, a trading name of James Constant, who for the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018 is the data controller.

To contact us about this policy or about your data, email support@picklesoft.co.uk.

2. What data we collect

None. We do not collect, receive or store any personal data about users of this app.

The app makes no requests to any server of ours. Every club, every season and every final league table is included in the app itself when you download it from the App Store. Because there is no data service to contact, your IP address is never seen or logged by us, and no record of your use of the app exists anywhere but on your own device.

There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no advertising identifier, no analytics and no third-party tracking software of any kind. The app does not build a profile of you and does not track you across other apps or websites.

3. What is stored on your device

Three things are saved on your own phone, in the app's private storage:

  • the club you support, so the app opens on it
  • which clubs you have compared it against, so your free comparisons are counted
  • whether you have unlocked the full version

None of this is transmitted to us, backed up to us, or readable by us. It is deleted when you delete the app. There is nothing for us to access, correct or erase because we never hold it.

4. Payment

The full version is a one-off purchase, not a subscription. The transaction is handled entirely by the Apple App Store under Apple's own privacy policy. We never see, receive or store your payment card details, your name, or your Apple Account, and no purchase information is sent to us.

5. Motion

The app can serve you a different line when you shake the phone. While a screen using that gesture is open it reads the device's accelerometer, solely to notice a shake. No motion data is recorded, stored or transmitted, and none of it leaves your phone. iOS will show you the reason for this access the first time it is used.

6. Notifications

The app sends no notifications and never asks for notification permission, so no push token exists and none is stored.

7. Children

The app is rated suitable for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children, and contains nothing age-restricted.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent.

In the case of this app there is nothing to exercise those rights against, because no personal data is collected or held. We have set them out so you know where you stand rather than to imply otherwise. If you believe we hold data about you, email support@picklesoft.co.uk and we will respond within one month.

If you are unsatisfied with how a request is handled, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

9. Other services we operate

PickleSoft publishes other apps and websites, which have their own privacy policies and their own data practices. This policy covers Bigger Than You only. Using this app does not connect you to, or share anything with, anything else we run.

10. Changes

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy — for example if a service were added to keep a purchase working across devices — this page will be updated before that change ships, and the date at the top will say when.

Bigger Than You is an independent statistical record. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any football club, league or governing body. Club names are used to identify historical results and nothing more.
