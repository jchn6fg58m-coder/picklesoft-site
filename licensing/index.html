Licensing the data

Last updated 10 November 2026

FC Score and Net Spend are free to use. The data and the method behind them are also available to license, for organisations that want football's finances read the same way for every club, every year.

What is available

FC Score: each club's financial condition

Every club scored out of 100 from its own annual accounts as filed at Companies House, and placed in one of six bands from Crisis Club to Model Club. Behind each score are ten measures across the operating business, the balance sheet and the transfer market (the academy included), each with the figures it is scored on, year by year, and the rule that scored it. Editions run year on year, so a club's movement can be followed and explained.

The accounts, read to one standard

Five years of each club's accounts on consistent house rules: revenue and its parts, wages, operating costs, the operating result, debt and what is owed to other clubs for players, and the one-off items that flatter a result, such as profit booked from an owner's own companies or sales to a club with the same owner. Every figure is traceable to the report it came from.

Net Spend: the transfer record

Every fee each club has paid and received since 2020-21, with the season and window, the other club, loan fees, and what became of each signing. From it: net spend season by season, the return on players bought and sold on, how much of a club's spending it later gets back, and who trades with whom, including the moves between clubs with the same owner.

The academy record

Each club's academy players and how their time ended: in the first team, sold for a fee, or released. Players are recorded as home-grown, purchased from another club, or from another club's youth set-up, so a club's own development can be told apart from the prospects it buys.

The method

The scoring method can be applied to other leagues and competitions, or to a set of clubs of your choosing, and the house rules for reading accounts can be shared with your own analysts.

Who it is for

Publishers and broadcasters, for graphics, explainers and match-week features. Analysts and football finance consultants. Advisers, lenders and investors looking at clubs. Data and fantasy products. Universities and researchers. Clubs and supporters' trusts that want an outside read of their own numbers.

How it works

Data is delivered as files or a regular feed, refreshed as accounts are filed and each transfer window closes, by arrangement. Licences cover editorial use, internal analysis or use inside a product, with credit to FC Score or Net Spend where the data is shown. Terms and fees depend on the use; tell us what you have in mind and we will put a proposal together.
