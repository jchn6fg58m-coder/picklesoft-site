FC Score

Privacy policy

Last updated 7 November 2026

FC Score collects nothing about you. It asks for nothing, and the only thing it sends is a request for the latest published scores. This page explains that in full, and sets out your rights under UK data protection law.

The short version. No account. No analytics. No adverts. No tracking. No notifications. The app downloads the published scores and figures when you open it, and nothing you do in it is sent anywhere.

1. Who we are

FC Score is an independent app that scores English football clubs on their financial health, from each club's own published accounts and its record of transfer fees. It is made with Two-Footed Tackle, the football finance newsletter, and published by PickleSoft, a trading name of James Constant, who for the purposes of the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018 is the data controller.

To contact us about this policy or about your data, email support@picklesoft.co.uk.

2. What data we collect

None. We do not collect, receive or store any personal data about users of this app.

To show you the scores, the app reads one published set of figures (each club's score and the numbers behind it) from our data service, which is hosted by Supabase. That request asks for the figures and nothing else: it carries no account, no name, no device identifier and nothing about what you look at in the app. The same figures go to everyone.

Like any server, the data service briefly records the IP address a request came from, for the purposes of running the service and protecting it from abuse. We do not use those records to identify you, link them to anything else, or keep them beyond the hosting provider's standard log period.

There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no advertising identifier, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking software of any kind. The app does not build a profile of you and does not track you across other apps or websites.

3. What is stored on your device

One thing is saved on your own phone, in the app's private storage: whether you have chosen not to see the cover screen when the app opens.

The scores you have looked at are kept in the app's memory while it is open, so pages load quickly, and are gone when it closes. None of this is transmitted to us or readable by us, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

4. Links to sources

Some background notes in the app list their sources, such as news reports. Tapping one opens it in your browser, outside the app, where that website's own privacy policy applies.

5. Payment

FC Score is free. There is nothing to buy in the app.

6. Notifications

The app sends no notifications and never asks for notification permission, so no push token exists and none is stored.

7. Children

The app is rated suitable for all ages. It collects no data from anyone, including children, and contains nothing age-restricted.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent.

In the case of this app there is nothing to exercise those rights against, because no personal data is collected or held. We have set them out so you know where you stand rather than to imply otherwise. If you believe we hold data about you, email support@picklesoft.co.uk and we will respond within one month.

If you are unsatisfied with how a request is handled, you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

9. Other services we operate

PickleSoft publishes other apps and websites, and Two-Footed Tackle publishes its newsletter; each has its own privacy policy. This policy covers FC Score only. Using this app does not sign you up to, or share anything with, anything else we run.

10. Changes

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy — for example if accounts, notifications or purchases were added — this page will be updated before that change ships, and the date at the top will say when.

FC Score is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any football club, league or governing body. Club names are used to identify published accounts and nothing more. FC Score is not financial or investment advice.
