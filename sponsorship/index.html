FC Score · Net Spend

Sponsoring the apps

Last updated 10 November 2026

FC Score and Net Spend are free apps about football's money: what clubs earn, spend and owe, and who buys and sells well. Each takes one sponsor at a time, by season or by transfer window.

What a sponsor gets

Your name across the app

An "In association with" strip carrying your name, a line of your choosing and a link, at the foot of the club pages, rankings and detail screens where the banner adverts would otherwise sit.

On every card people share

Both apps turn a club's figures into a card to send: a club's score, its transfer receipt, a ranking, or two clubs side by side. Every card carries your name as it travels through group chats and social feeds, alongside the app and its App Store badge.

The adverts switched off

While you are the sponsor, the network adverts are switched off for every reader. Yours is the only brand in the app.

When it changes

A sponsorship starts and ends on the day agreed, with no app update needed. One app or both.

Who it suits

Brands that want to stand beside football's money story and the people who follow it closely: kit and boot makers, finance and fintech, publishers, data and analytics firms, agencies, and anyone with a reason to be next to an independent, figures-first read of the game.

The apps

FC Score scores every Premier League club out of 100 on its financial condition, from its own published accounts, and follows each club edition by edition. Net Spend is every club's transfer receipt: what went out, what came in, and the total at the bottom, for any run of seasons since 2020-21. Both are by Two-Footed Tackle, built by PickleSoft.
