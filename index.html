Our first game · iPhone

Horologist

From a rented bench on Vyse Street to the finest watch in the world.

  • Start in Birmingham's Jewellery Quarter with a borrowed bench and a bank overdraft. Batteries and bracelets first; tourbillons, one day.
  • Every repair is hands-on: open the caseback, size the bracelet, regulate on the timing machine. Good work builds trust; poor work comes back.
  • Grow the workshop: hire and train staff, win over the Quarter's dealers and collectors, and design watches of your own.
  • Your career ends with your masterpiece, valued and ranked against other players' best.
In development · test version coming soon

AVDB

Aston Villa's history, every match and every player.

  • Every match: the line-ups, the report, and a timeline of the goals, cards and substitutions.
  • Every player: a career season by season and competition by competition, with the illustrations.
  • The same archive as avdb.co.uk, so anything added to the site shows up in the app.

Every English club, scored on where it finished, 1888–89 to 2025–26.
