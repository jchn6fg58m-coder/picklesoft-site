Our first game · iPhone

Horologist

From a rented bench on Vyse Street to the finest watch in the world.

  • Start in Birmingham's Jewellery Quarter with a borrowed bench and a bank overdraft. Batteries and bracelets first; tourbillons, one day.
  • Every repair is hands-on: open the caseback, size the bracelet, regulate on the timing machine. Good work builds trust; poor work comes back.
  • Grow the workshop: hire and train staff, win over the Quarter's dealers and collectors, and design watches of your own.
  • Your career ends with your masterpiece, valued and ranked against other players' best.
In development · test version coming soon

Two-Footed Tackle · iPhone

FC Score

Every Premier League club, scored on its financial condition.

  • Ten measures from each club's own published accounts and transfer record, rolled into one score out of 100 and a band from red to green.
  • Three editions, from the 2022-23 accounts to 2024-25, so you can see who is getting better and who is getting worse.
  • A written verdict on every club, and the top three and bottom three on every measure.
  • Share any club as a card, made for the group chat.

Two-Footed Tackle · iPhone

Net Spend

Every club's transfers as a receipt: what went out, and what came back.

  • Pick a season, or a run of them from 2020-21: who spends, who spends badly, who sells well and whose academy delivers.
  • Every club's receipt: a red line for money out and a green line for money in, drawn against each other.
  • Tap any figure for the players behind it.
  • Put two clubs' receipts side by side and share the comparison.
