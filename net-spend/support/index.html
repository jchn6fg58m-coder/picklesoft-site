Reporting a fee you think is wrong

Every fee comes from Two-Footed Tackle's record of each club's transfers, recorded under the same rules for every club. If you think one is wrong, tell us the player, the club, the season and what you believe the fee was, with a source if you have one. Corrections are checked and published as soon as they are confirmed, with no app update needed.

My club isn't in the app yet

Clubs are added as each club's transfers are checked. A club appears the moment it is published; you do not need to update the app.

How is net spend worked out?

Spent is every transfer fee and loan fee a club paid for players signed in the seasons you choose; received is every fee and loan fee it was paid for players who left in them. Net spend is the difference. Pick a run of seasons, or a single one, at the top of any screen, and every figure adds up for those seasons.

What do the red and green lines mean?

Each club's receipt has a red line for what it spent and a green line for what it received. The longer of the two fills the width and the other is drawn as its share, so the shape shows at a glance whether a club is a spender or a seller; the total at the bottom says by how much. When two clubs are compared, both receipts are drawn on one scale.

Return on players sold, and money back

Return on players sold looks at players bought for a fee who left in the seasons chosen: what they fetched against what they cost, with a player released counting as fetching nothing. Money back follows the other way: the players signed in the seasons chosen, and how much of what was paid for them has come back in fees since. Both use transfer fees alone, without loan fees.

Home-grown, purchased and youth team

Academy players are recorded as home-grown (no other club in their history), purchased (joined directly from another club, for a fee or free) or youth team (trained at another club first, but did not join directly from it). A player counts in the seasons in which his time at the club ended, or once he has reached the first team.

Why are fees in euros?

Transfer fees are recorded in euros, as they are most widely reported, at the fixed fee agreed at the time of the deal. A fee agreed in pounds is converted at its season's rate.

Compare, and the advert

Compare sets two clubs' receipts side by side. It opens for the rest of the day after one short advert of your choosing. When adverts are switched off or the app has a sponsor, it is simply open.

Why are there adverts?

Net Spend is free, and adverts help keep it that way. They come from Google, sit at the foot of pages and in the rankings, and a full-screen one appears at most once a visit. You choose on first opening whether Google may personalise them, and can change that with Ad privacy choices at the foot of the home screen. When the app has a season sponsor, the adverts are switched off.

Is this the Premier League's spending rules?

No. Net Spend adds up transfer fees paid and received. It is not the Premier League's profit and sustainability calculation, which spreads a fee over a player's contract, and it says nothing about whether a club has met those rules.

The app shows nothing, or an error

Net Spend needs an internet connection to load the latest figures. Check you are online and open the app again. If it still fails, email us with what the screen says.
