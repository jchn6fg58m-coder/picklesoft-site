Reporting a mistake in the data

The record runs to 213 clubs across 127 seasons, and it is compiled by hand from final league tables. If you think something is wrong, please say which club, which season, and what it should be. Corrections are taken seriously and applied at the next update.

My club's colours are wrong

Club colours are chosen to identify a club on screen, which is not always its shirt: a white-shirted club is shown in its trim, because white on white is nothing. If yours looks wrong to you, tell us and we will look at it.

How do the six free comparisons work?

You get six different opponents, not six taps. Going back to a club you have already looked at costs nothing and never will, so nobody loses one to a mis-tap. Random tie does not count against the six either, because you cannot aim a random draw at the club you actually wanted. Your own club is never an opponent.

Shaking the phone does nothing

Shake works on the Taunt tab and serves a different line each time. The line under the card tells you which is available: if it reads "Tap Another" rather than "Shake for another", your device has not made its motion sensor available to the app — the Another button does exactly the same job.

I paid and the app still says I have used my free comparisons

Open the app and use Restore purchases. The App Store holds the record of your purchase, so restoring it on the same Apple Account will reopen everything. If that does not work, email us with the date of purchase and we will help.

Why does it say my club finished 40th?

Positions are counted across the whole English pyramid rather than within a division, so a club that won the second tier is shown as around 21st in the country. That is what makes seasons comparable across eras and divisions. The app explains each measure behind the information button next to it.

Requesting a refund

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.
