Reporting a figure you think is wrong

Every figure is read from the club's own annual accounts as filed at Companies House, or from its record of transfer fees, and recorded under the same rules for every club. If you think one is wrong, tell us the club, the edition, the measure and what you believe it should be, with the page of the accounts if you have it. Corrections are checked against the accounts and published as soon as they are confirmed, with no app update needed.

My club isn't scored yet

Clubs are added as each one's accounts and transfer record are checked. A club appears on the table the moment it is published; you do not need to update the app. Pull down on the table to refresh.

Why did my club get the score it did?

Tap the club, then any of the ten measures. Each one shows what the club scored, the steps it is scored on with its own step highlighted, and the figures behind it, year by year. How a club is scored, at the foot of the table, sets out the method in full.

What are the editions?

Each edition scores the clubs on one year's accounts and the transfer windows around it. Edition 3, 2025-26, uses the 2024-25 accounts. Switch between editions with the tabs at the top of any screen to see how a club has moved.

Why are transfer fees in euros?

Transfer fees are recorded in euros, as they are most widely reported, at the fixed fee agreed at the time of the deal. Where they are set against revenue, the revenue is converted at that season’s exchange rate. Everything from the accounts is in pounds.

Is this the Premier League's spending rules?

No. FC Score is an independent measure of financial health. It is not the Premier League's profit and sustainability calculation, and a club's score says nothing about whether it has met those rules. Where the app refers to what the spending rules count, it says so.

Why are there adverts?

FC Score is free, and adverts help keep it that way. They come from Google, sit at the foot of pages and in the table, and a full-screen one appears at most once a visit. You choose on first opening whether Google may personalise them, and can change that with Ad privacy choices at the foot of the table. When the app has a season sponsor, the adverts are switched off.

The app shows nothing, or an error

FC Score needs an internet connection to load the latest scores. Check you are online, then pull down on the table to try again. If it still fails, email us with what the screen says.

Privacy

The app collects nothing about you itself. It can show adverts from Google, with your consent where the law requires it; Ad privacy choices, at the foot of the table, lets you change that at any time. The full explanation is in the privacy policy.
