Is Horologist free?

The first chapter, running your own repair workshop, is free to play in full. A single one-off purchase unlocks the rest of the game. There are no adverts in your way, no in-game currency and no way to pay for an advantage.

I've changed phones. How do I get my purchase back?

Open Settings in the game and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Where is my game saved?

On your iPhone. If you delete the app, your save goes with it.

Are the businesses in the game real?

Most are invented, including every character. Real Jewellery Quarter businesses only appear as clearly labelled partners, as themselves.
